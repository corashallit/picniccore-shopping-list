## How to author a key

- Draw it in the DSL in the batch's own file (`design/stickers/icons_batch1.py` ... `icons_batch4.py`, loaded by `icons.py` after the r9 files), with `add('<catalog key>', '<label>', [parts], qty=..., aisle=...)`. A sticker whose DSL key is its catalog key needs no entry in `catalog_map.py`. Never hand-place gloss: `gloss.autogloss` draws it.
- Follow §3.1: 52-unit box, art in 6–42 with the base on y 42, colour-trace outlines (2u), at most two families plus white and lemon pastel, the puffy bottom band, no text, no brands. The drawHint below is what the live pipeline would have drawn; match its subject, not its colours.
- Run `python3 design/stickers/build_android.py` (emits `sk_i_<key>.xml` and the manifests, runs the validators: a new key gets no grandfathering), then `python3 design/stickers/tools/roundtrip.py <key>` and review the contact sheet at 52 / 30 / 22 dp on the Day, Glow and Dusk binders before merge (§18.3 step 4, §20 item 34).
- Re-use the r9 donors: `pretzel` (an orphan, kept in the DSL) for `snacks_generic`; `kibble` (dropped as a duplicate of cat food) can seed `dog_food` with a dog on the bag.
